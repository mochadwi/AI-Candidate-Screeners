# CV Match Evaluation Rubric

## Scoring Scale: 1-5 per parameter

## Parameters and Weights

### 1. Technical Skills Match (Weight: 40%)

**Description:** Alignment with job requirements (backend, databases, APIs, cloud, AI/LLM)

**Scoring Guide:**
- **1 = Irrelevant skills:** No relevant technical skills for the role
- **2 = Few overlaps:** Some technical skills but mostly irrelevant
- **3 = Partial match:** Decent technical foundation, some gaps in key areas
- **4 = Strong match:** Most required technical skills present
- **5 = Excellent match + AI/LLM exposure:** All required skills plus AI/LLM experience

**Key Skills to Evaluate:**
- Backend frameworks (Node.js, Django, Rails, etc.)
- Database technologies (SQL, NoSQL)
- API development (RESTful, GraphQL)
- Cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure)
- AI/LLM technologies (embeddings, vector DBs, prompt engineering, RAG)
- Server-side languages (JavaScript, Python, Ruby, Java, etc.)

---

### 2. Experience Level (Weight: 25%)

**Description:** Years of experience and project complexity

**Scoring Guide:**
- **1 = <1 year / trivial projects:** Less than 1 year experience or only basic projects
- **2 = 1-2 years:** Junior level with some real-world experience
- **3 = 2-3 years with mid-scale projects:** Mid-level with moderate complexity projects
- **4 = 3-4 years solid track record:** Senior-level with proven track record
- **5 = 5+ years / high-impact projects:** Very senior with significant impact

**Evaluation Criteria:**
- Total years of relevant backend experience
- Complexity and scale of projects worked on
- Progression of responsibilities over time
- Variety of technologies and domains

---

### 3. Relevant Achievements (Weight: 20%)

**Description:** Impact of past work (scaling, performance, adoption)

**Scoring Guide:**
- **1 = No clear achievements:** No measurable outcomes mentioned
- **2 = Minimal improvements:** Minor improvements or contributions
- **3 = Some measurable outcomes:** Clear but modest achievements
- **4 = Significant contributions:** Notable impact on systems or processes
- **5 = Major measurable impact:** Exceptional achievements with quantified results

**Look for:**
- Performance improvements (e.g., "reduced API latency by 50%")
- Scalability achievements (e.g., "handled 10x traffic increase")
- User adoption metrics (e.g., "feature used by 1M+ users")
- System reliability improvements
- Cost optimizations
- Innovation and problem-solving impact

---

### 4. Cultural / Collaboration Fit (Weight: 15%)

**Description:** Communication, learning mindset, teamwork/leadership

**Scoring Guide:**
- **1 = Not demonstrated:** No evidence of soft skills or collaboration
- **2 = Minimal:** Limited evidence of collaboration or communication
- **3 = Average:** Standard collaboration and communication skills
- **4 = Good:** Clear evidence of strong teamwork and communication
- **5 = Excellent and well-demonstrated:** Outstanding collaboration, leadership, and growth mindset

**Evaluation Criteria:**
- Communication skills (technical writing, documentation)
- Cross-functional collaboration (frontend, product, design)
- Learning attitude (new technologies, continuous improvement)
- Leadership or mentorship experience
- Open source contributions or community involvement
- Remote work experience (for remote roles)

---

## Overall CV Match Rate Calculation

**Formula:**
```
Weighted Average = (Technical Skills × 0.4) + (Experience × 0.25) + (Achievements × 0.2) + (Cultural Fit × 0.15)
CV Match Rate = Weighted Average × 0.2
```

**Result:** 0-1 decimal scale (e.g., 0.82 means 82% match)

## Evaluation Tips
- Focus on actual skills and achievements, not just job titles
- Consider relevance to the specific role requirements
- Look for concrete examples and measurable results
- Evaluate learning trajectory and growth potential
- Consider both breadth and depth of experience
